Privacy Policy

WorldCup Testnet · Last updated 28 October 2026

WorldCup (“we”, “the app”) is an international football app for the Pi Network. This policy explains what information WorldCup uses and why. We keep it to the minimum the app needs.

1. Information we use

  • Pi account (only if you sign in): your Pi username and a Pi user ID, provided by Pi Network when you choose “Sign in with Pi”. We use them to show your name and to process Pi payments. We do not receive your password, wallet passphrase or KYC details.
  • Pi payments (only if you pay): the payment ID, amount, blockchain transaction ID and your Pi user ID, so that the payment can be approved and completed with Pi Network.
  • Saved on your device only: the teams and competitions you follow, recently viewed matches and your settings (such as 24-hour clock). These stay in your browser storage and are not sent to us.

2. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking or analytics tools, and no selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No email, phone number or location is collected.

3. Service providers

  • Pi Network — sign-in and payments, under Pi Network’s own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare — hosts WorldCup. Like any web host it processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the app. Short-lived server logs record sign-in and payment steps (Pi user ID, username, payment and transaction IDs) so we can fix problems; they are not used for anything else.
  • Football data providers (football-data.org and API-Football) — our server requests match data from them. No personal information about you is sent.
  • Google Fonts — the scoreboard font is loaded from Google, which receives standard technical data such as your IP address.

4. Your choices

  • You can use WorldCup without signing in.
  • “Sign out” in My Football removes your Pi session from the app. Clearing the app’s data in your browser removes your follows and settings.
  • For any privacy question or request, email support.worldcup@gmail.com.

5. Children

WorldCup shows general football information and is not directed at children. Pi Network sets its own age requirements for Pi accounts.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
